How it works

Travel-powered delivery

RunWise turns journeys into opportunities — for everyone.

One app. Two ways to use it. Send what you need, or earn as a Runner on journeys you're already making.

📦

Send what you need

Shopping, parcels, documents, gifts, business stock or large cargo — if it fits, we can move it.

🚗

Already travelling?

Announce your route and earn money carrying items along the way. Turn your next trip into income.

🔒

Safe & tracked

Escrow payments, delivery PINs, live journey milestones, and photo proof — for every order.

🌍

Cross-border ready

Connect across Botswana, South Africa, Zimbabwe and Zambia. Cross-border deliveries are matched with travellers already making the journey. Senders and runners remain responsible for applicable customs requirements.

⭐

Trusted runners

Runners complete identity verification, with additional transport or vehicle verification where applicable. Ratings and RunScore help build trust across the community.

💬

Support & dispute resolution

Dedicated support, dispute resolution, emergency procedures, and next-of-kin contact for every active order.
